A Plus Family HealthCare - Call Routing (V5 Site-Only Model)

Site-only routing: Each number goes to its own inbound route user (8910, 8911, 8912). That user checks the clinic's own time frame. If the clinic is open, the call goes to that clinic's auto attendant and queue, and only that clinic's phones ring. If it is closed, the call goes to Main Routing User 8903 and then After-Hours AA 8163.
Ring order: Order 1 primary rings for 90 seconds, then the Order 2 failover group is added. No voicemail; unanswered callers stay in the queue.
Hours: Brownsville Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 8 AM - 5 PM CT, Wed 7 AM - 4 PM CT. Sonora and Elizabethtown Mon-Fri 8 AM - 5 PM ET (7 AM - 4 PM CT). No lunch closure.
